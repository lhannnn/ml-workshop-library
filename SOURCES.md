## Public proposal attachments

The five PDFs were downloaded from the [ICLR 2025 Workshop Proposals — Accept collection](https://openreview.net/group?id=ICLR.cc/2025/Workshop_Proposals#tab-accept). The collection notes recorded all five as visible to everyone at download time. Each PDF has its own forum link, attachment URL, and checksum in the [manifest](data/proposals.json).

The files preserve the attachments downloaded on the snapshot date. Their earliest submission versions and revision histories have not been independently established. Public access can differ by conference, year, and submission.

## Supplementary coverage

The [additional event index](catalog/additional-events.md) contains 34 community and Expo entries found in the original collection. These are not included in the main total.

- The ICML 2025 community list had an unresolved Queer in AI event-format/access gap.
- A complete NeurIPS 2026 Expo workshop directory was not obtained.
- “Geometry-adaptive Hyperbolic Learning in Computer Vision” was excluded from the workshop count because its event page identified it as an Oral paper presentation.
- EurIPS 2025 is outside the scope of this three-conference catalogue.
